This space collects short, focused writeups on **recent machine-learning
techniques** alongside my own **hands-on application notes**. Unlike the
[blog](/log/blog/) (paper reviews and study notes) or
[reports](/log/reports/) (long-form analyses), entries here are meant to be
practical and reproducible:

1. **What is the technique?** A one-paragraph framing of the idea — what
   problem it solves and how it differs from prior work.
2. **My experiment.** Code, prompt, or configuration I tried, and what
   actually happened.
3. **Takeaways.** What I would (or would not) reuse, and the open questions
   that remain.

Posts are written in English to make them easier to share with collaborators.
